Founder and Director

Christopher Hoy

Christopher Hoy is an applied microeconomist specializing in measuring and understanding people’s beliefs and preferences about economic policies. His research has been published in leading journals, including the American Economic Journal: Economic Policy, featured by The New York Times, The Economist and the BBC, and cited around 2,500 times.
